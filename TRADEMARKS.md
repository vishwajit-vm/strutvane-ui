**Strutvane™** and the Strutvane logo are trademarks of **Vishwajit Mall** (decision #136). Registration is in progress (not yet registered). This policy explains what you may do with the name. ## The code: MIT
Under the MIT licence you may use, copy, change, merge, publish, distribute, sublicense and sell the code, including in commercial products. You must keep the copyright notice and licence text. Nothing in this policy limits those rights.

## The name and logo: ask first for anything that could confuse people
**Allowed without asking:**
- Saying that your product is **built with**, **uses** or **works with** Strutvane, for example "Built with Strutvane".
- Writing about Strutvane in articles, tutorials, talks, courses and reviews.
- Naming a community package `strutvane-<something>`, as long as its README says clearly that it is not an official Strutvane package.
- Showing the unmodified logo next to such a statement.

**Needs written permission from the trademark owner:**
- Calling a fork, a distribution, a product, a company, a service or a domain name "Strutvane", or something confusingly similar.
- Publishing under the `@strutvane` npm scope or the `strutvane-` GitHub names used by the official project.
- Suggesting that your product is official, endorsed, certified or supported by Strutvane.
- Changing the logo, or using it as part of your own logo.

**If you fork the code:** you're welcome to, under MIT. Give your fork a different name and logo, and say that it's based on Strutvane.
